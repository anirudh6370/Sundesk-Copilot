# Aravalli Solar Works — Company Overview

Aravalli Solar Works is a fictional rooftop solar company used as the sample customer for this project. All names, prices, codes and policies in this knowledge base are invented for demonstration.

## What We Do

Aravalli Solar Works designs, installs and maintains rooftop solar systems for homes, housing societies and small commercial buildings. We handle the full journey: site survey, system design, installation, commissioning, net-metering paperwork, monitoring and after-sales service.

## Service Regions

We currently operate in four service regions, each with its own field team and warehouse:

- North Region: hub in Jaipur
- West Region: hub in Pune
- South Region: hub in Coimbatore
- Central Region: hub in Indore

Installations outside a 120 km radius of a regional hub are accepted only after approval from the Regional Operations Manager, and a remote-site surcharge applies.

## System Packages

| Package | Capacity | Typical customer | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| SunStart | 2 kW to 3 kW | Small homes | HelioBox Lite |
| SunHome | 4 kW to 6 kW | Family homes | HelioBox |
| SunPlus | 7 kW to 10 kW | Large homes, small shops | HelioBox |
| SunBiz | 11 kW to 50 kW | Societies, small commercial | HelioBox Pro |

Every package includes panels, an inverter, mounting structure, DC and AC protection boxes, earthing, a lightning arrester and the HelioBox monitoring gateway. Battery storage (the "SunVault" add-on) is optional and is available only with SunHome and larger packages.

## Customer Support Channels

- Sun app: customers raise and track service requests from the Aravalli Sun mobile app. This is the preferred channel.
- Support line: open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- Emergency line: open 24 hours, every day, for safety issues only (burning smell, sparks, visible damage after a storm, water inside an electrical box).
- Email: used for documents, invoices and warranty paperwork. Emails are answered within one working day.

## Ticket Priorities

| Priority | Meaning | First response | Field visit target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Safety risk or fire hazard | 15 minutes | Within 4 hours |
| P2 | System producing no power | 2 hours | Within 2 working days |
| P3 | Reduced generation or monitoring offline | 1 working day | Within 5 working days |
| P4 | Billing, paperwork or general question | 1 working day | Not applicable |

## Internal Teams

- Sales and Survey: site surveys, proposals and contracts.
- Projects: design, procurement and installation.
- Service Desk: first-line customer support and ticket triage.
- Field Service: on-site diagnosis, repair and annual maintenance.
- Grid Liaison: net-metering applications and coordination with the electricity distribution company.
- Billing: invoices, payment plans and refunds.

## Escalation

If a ticket misses its field visit target, the Service Desk escalates it to the Regional Service Lead. If it is still unresolved two working days later, it is escalated to the Head of Customer Operations. Customers must be told about each escalation in the Sun app.
