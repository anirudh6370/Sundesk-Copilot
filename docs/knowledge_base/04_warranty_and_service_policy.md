# Aravalli Solar Works — Warranty and Service Policy

## Warranty Coverage

| Component | Warranty period | What is covered |
|---|---|---|
| Solar panels (product) | 12 years | Manufacturing defects |
| Solar panels (performance) | 25 years | Output below 80 percent of rated power |
| Inverter | 8 years | Manufacturing defects and internal faults |
| Mounting structure | 10 years | Corrosion and structural failure |
| HelioBox gateway | 3 years | Hardware failure |
| SunVault battery | 7 years or 4,000 cycles, whichever comes first | Capacity below 70 percent of rated |
| Installation workmanship | 5 years | Cabling, earthing, roof penetrations and leakage caused by our work |

Warranty periods start on the commissioning date shown in the handover pack.

## What Is Not Covered

- Damage from lightning strikes, flooding, cyclones, hail or fire that did not start in our equipment. Customers are advised to add the system to their home insurance.
- Damage caused by animals, including monkeys and rodents, unless the customer bought the optional mesh guard.
- Repairs or modifications by anyone other than Aravalli Field Service.
- Panels broken by impact, such as a cricket ball or falling branches.
- Reduced generation caused by new shading or by panels that have not been cleaned.
- Systems moved to a different roof without an Aravalli relocation service.

## Raising a Warranty Claim

1. The customer raises a service request in the Sun app and selects "Warranty".
2. The Service Desk checks the commissioning date and the component serial number.
3. A Field Service engineer inspects the system and records photos and readings.
4. If the claim is approved, the faulty part is repaired or replaced at no cost, including labour.
5. If the claim is rejected, the customer receives a written reason and a paid repair quote.

Approved inverter replacements are completed within 7 working days. While the customer waits, a loan inverter is provided if the outage is expected to exceed 10 working days.

## Annual Maintenance Plans

The first year of maintenance is free with every installation. After that, customers can choose a plan:

| Plan | Visits per year | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| CareBasic | 2 | Panel cleaning, visual inspection, earthing check |
| CarePlus | 4 | Everything in CareBasic plus string testing, thermal scan and priority support |
| CareTotal | 6 | Everything in CarePlus plus free replacement of out-of-warranty consumables such as fuses, surge protectors and MC4 connectors |

Customers without a plan pay a visit charge for each field visit that is not a warranty repair.

## Generation Guarantee

SunHome, SunPlus and SunBiz customers on a CarePlus or CareTotal plan receive a generation guarantee. If annual generation is below 90 percent of the estimate in the proposal, and the shortfall is not caused by shading, uncleaned panels or grid outages, Aravalli credits the shortfall at the customer's export tariff.

## Relocation and Removal

If a customer needs the system moved for roof repairs or a house move, Aravalli offers a paid relocation service. The workmanship warranty continues only if Aravalli performs the removal and reinstallation.

## Refunds and Cancellation

- Cancellation before material dispatch: the advance is refunded in full, less the site survey fee.
- Cancellation after dispatch but before installation: a restocking charge of 10 percent of the contract value applies.
- Cancellation after installation has started is not possible.

Refunds are processed within 10 working days of approval.
