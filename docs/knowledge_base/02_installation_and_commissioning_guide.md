# Aravalli Solar Works — Installation and Commissioning Guide

This guide describes how a rooftop project moves from signed contract to a working system. It is written for Projects and Field Service staff.

## Project Timeline

A standard residential project takes 21 to 30 working days from contract signing to commissioning:

1. Site survey: within 3 working days of the enquiry.
2. Design and proposal: within 2 working days of the survey.
3. Contract and advance payment: customer pays 30 percent to confirm the order.
4. Material dispatch: within 7 working days of the advance.
5. Installation: 2 to 4 days on site, depending on capacity.
6. Commissioning and handover: within 2 working days of installation.
7. Net-metering application: filed by Grid Liaison within 3 working days of commissioning.

The net meter itself is installed by the electricity distribution company, and that timeline is outside our control.

## Site Survey Checklist

The surveyor must record:

- Roof type: RCC flat roof, metal sheet or tiled. Asbestos sheet roofs are not accepted.
- Usable shadow-free area. We plan for roughly 10 square metres per kW on a flat roof.
- Shading from water tanks, parapets, trees and neighbouring buildings, checked for morning and afternoon.
- Roof condition and waterproofing. Any existing leakage must be photographed and shown to the customer before work begins.
- Sanctioned load and connection type (single phase or three phase) from the latest electricity bill.
- Cable route from roof to the main distribution board, and a location for the inverter that is shaded, ventilated and at least 1.2 metres above floor level.
- Earthing pit locations. Every system needs three separate earthing pits: DC side, AC side and lightning arrester.

## Installation Rules

- Mounting structures on RCC roofs use ballast or chemical anchors. Drilling through a waterproofed slab requires written customer consent.
- Panels are tilted between 10 and 20 degrees and face south wherever the roof allows.
- A walkway of at least 600 mm must be left for cleaning access.
- DC cables run in UV-resistant conduit. Loose cables across the roof are not allowed.
- The inverter must not be installed in direct sunlight, in a bathroom, or inside a closed cupboard.
- Systems above 5 kW require a three-phase connection. If the customer has a single-phase connection, the Projects team must advise a load enhancement before installation.

## Commissioning Checklist

The commissioning engineer completes these checks before handover:

1. Visual inspection of structure, clamps, cable glands and labels.
2. Earthing resistance measured at each pit. The reading must be below 5 ohms.
3. Insulation resistance test on each DC string. The reading must be above 1 megaohm.
4. Open-circuit voltage of each string compared with the design value. A difference above 5 percent must be investigated.
5. Inverter powered on and grid parameters confirmed.
6. Anti-islanding test: switch off the grid supply and confirm the inverter stops exporting within 2 seconds.
7. HelioBox gateway connected to the customer's Wi-Fi and showing live data in the Sun app.
8. Customer walkthrough: emergency shutdown, reading the inverter display, raising a ticket.

## Handover Documents

The customer receives a handover pack containing the system design, serial numbers of panels and inverter, warranty certificates, the commissioning report and the emergency shutdown card. The handover pack is also uploaded to the Sun app under "My Documents".

## Common Installation Delays

- Customer's sanctioned load is lower than the planned system capacity.
- Roof waterproofing work is pending.
- Society or landlord permission letter is missing.
- Monsoon weather: installation is paused during active rain and when wind speed makes roof work unsafe.
