# Aravalli Solar Works — Net Metering and Billing FAQ

This FAQ explains how Aravalli handles net metering and billing. Government subsidy amounts, tariff rates and distribution company rules change often and differ by state, so they are deliberately not listed here. Staff must check the current official source before quoting any figure to a customer.

## Net Metering

### What is net metering?

With net metering, the customer's meter records both the electricity taken from the grid and the surplus solar electricity sent to the grid. The electricity bill is based on the difference between the two.

### Who applies for the net meter?

Aravalli's Grid Liaison team prepares and files the application on the customer's behalf within 3 working days of commissioning. The electricity distribution company inspects the system and installs the net meter. Their timeline is outside Aravalli's control.

### What documents does the customer need to provide?

- Latest electricity bill
- Identity proof of the person named on the electricity connection
- Property ownership proof, or a no-objection letter from the owner or housing society
- A cancelled cheque or bank details, where the distribution company pays for exported units
- Signed application forms prepared by Grid Liaison

### Can the system run before the net meter is installed?

Yes, but exported units will not be credited until the net meter is in place. Customers are advised to use as much solar power as possible during the day in this period.

### Why is the system capacity limited?

Distribution companies limit the solar capacity to a share of the customer's sanctioned load. If the customer wants a bigger system, a load enhancement must be approved first. Grid Liaison can file this request, and it normally adds two to four weeks.

### My electricity bill has not dropped. Why?

Common reasons are: the net meter is not yet installed, the billing cycle started before commissioning, fixed charges still apply even when net units are zero, or household consumption has increased. Ask the customer for the latest bill and compare the import and export readings with the Sun app.

## Aravalli Billing

### Payment schedule

- 30 percent advance on signing the contract
- 60 percent before material dispatch
- 10 percent after commissioning

### Payment methods

Bank transfer, UPI, card payment through the Sun app, or an approved finance partner. Cash is not accepted for amounts above the site survey fee.

### Financing

Aravalli's finance partners offer instalment plans of 12 to 60 months for SunHome and larger packages. Approval is decided by the finance partner, not by Aravalli. If a loan is rejected after the advance is paid, the customer can pay directly or cancel under the normal cancellation policy.

### Subsidy

Where a government subsidy applies, Aravalli helps the customer register on the official portal and uploads the required installation documents. The subsidy is paid by the government directly to the customer's bank account. Aravalli does not deduct an expected subsidy from the invoice and cannot guarantee the amount or the date.

### Invoices and tax

A tax invoice is issued at each payment stage and stored in the Sun app under "My Documents". Requests to change the name or address on an invoice must be made before the final invoice is issued.

### Late payment

If the final 10 percent is unpaid 15 days after commissioning, remote monitoring support is paused and a reminder is sent. Warranty cover is not removed for late payment, but non-warranty field visits are put on hold until the account is settled.

## Who to Contact

- Net-metering status: Grid Liaison, through the "Net Meter" tracker in the Sun app.
- Invoice or payment question: Billing team, P4 ticket.
- Subsidy status: check the official portal first, then raise a P4 ticket if documents from Aravalli are pending.
